Biochemical Fundamentals

Peptide Synthesis & Molecular Structure

Understanding amino acid linkages, solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS), and secondary folding.

Amino Acid Chains

Peptides consist of short chains of 2 to 50 amino acids linked by amide/peptide bonds formed between carboxylic and amino groups.

SPPS Synthesis

Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis allows stepwise assembly of peptide chains attached to an insoluble polymeric resin support, ensuring high yield and purity control.

Lyophilization & Storage

Lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder maintains molecular stability at -20°C for extended periods. Reconstituted solutions require 2-8°C refrigeration.
